## What boot 5 found instead: no leader chord works from a USB keyboard

`scancodes=147 chords=0`. The operator reached a guest with the `focus` command and had
no way back out.

`hype_chord_feed_scancode()` sets `right_ctrl_held` / `right_alt_held` only from the
EXTENDED forms `E0 1D` and `E0 38`, and every chord needs both. The USB path emitted bare
0x1D and 0x38, folded onto the left-hand codes. No chord could ever fire. Arrows were
un-prefixed too, so they did nothing in hype's own line editor either -- un-prefixed 0x48
is the keypad, and `core/kbd_decode.c` only maps arrows behind the prefix.

Fixed in `a6ad16c`: the USB path emits real Set-1. Right Shift stays single-byte, because
on Set-1 it genuinely is.

**"The keyboard froze when the VM froze" was this, not a freeze.** Input kept flowing for
the whole minute after the guest wedged on `reboot` at t=190.5s -- reports 130 -> 151 and
scancodes 129 -> 147 by t=248s, a steady ~90 polls/sec. What was lost was the way OUT:
focus was on a dead guest, every keystroke went to it, and Right-Ctrl+Right-Alt+Esc could
not be assembled. The wedge itself is #735 and still has no fix.

### Try these, in this order

1. **Move the mouse.** Boot 5 never moved it, so `MOUSE reports=0 errors=0` proved only
   that it is not erroring. One movement settles it.
2. **Right-Ctrl + Right-Alt + D** -- toggle the dashboard.
3. **Right-Ctrl + Right-Alt + 1** -- jump to VM 1.
4. **Right-Ctrl + Right-Alt + Esc** -- back to the dashboard. This is the one that was
   missing when you were stuck in the guest.
5. **Right-Ctrl + Right-Alt + Left / Right** -- cycle VMs.
6. **Arrow keys at the dashboard prompt** -- they should now move the cursor. They never
   did before from USB.
7. Then let the guest reach its `reboot` and wedge as usual, and **from inside the wedged
   guest, press Right-Ctrl + Right-Alt + Esc**. Getting back to a live dashboard out of a
   dead guest is the real test.

Note the chord needs the **right-hand** Ctrl and Alt specifically. On a keyboard with no
right Alt (or where it is a Fn/AltGr key), the chord cannot be typed at all -- say so if
that is the case here and it becomes a ticket of its own.
